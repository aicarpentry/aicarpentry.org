---
title: AI Carpentry Governance
---

AI Carpentry currently has no formal governance structure, and is led by consensus among the members of working groups focused on the development of particular workshops. 
The project is supported by The Carpentries Curriculum Team.

In the medium term, we aim to appoint a Governance Committee to steer the project, and Curriculum Advisory Committees to guide workshop development.
These goals align with our overall aim to become an official Lesson Program of The Carpentries.
